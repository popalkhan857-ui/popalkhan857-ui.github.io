About Me

Professional cardiovascular perfusion profile

I am a Clinical Cardiovascular Perfusionist dedicated to providing safe, precise and effective perfusion support during cardiac surgery.

I have experience supporting both adult and pediatric cardiac surgical procedures and continuously work to improve my clinical knowledge and technical skills.
